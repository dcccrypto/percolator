The gap: Kani's proofs are per step (inductive, scalar) or per bounded world. What is missing is that every public entry point preserves the senior leg for every state satisfying the global invariant, with any number of accounts and assets. `validate_shape` checks the senior leg at the end of most entry points at RUN time; this task proves it statically, so the run-time check is never the thing that saves the books.
